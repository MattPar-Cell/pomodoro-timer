What is this?

An intentionally over-engineered pomodoro timer. It has more features than anyone could ever need — that's the point. Several of them are powered by tiny interpreters for gloriously underrated languages, all running right here in your browser with zero dependencies:

Forth 🧮 automations Lisp λ rules Array-lang 📊 stats Brainfuck 🧠 easter egg raw WebAssembly 🕸 counter

Open the λ REPL tab to poke at the interpreters. Press ⌘K for the command palette. Everything persists locally; nothing is uploaded unless you flip on Sync and run the FastAPI backend.
